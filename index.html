Summary Information

FAO Area 51 (Arabian Sea)

The Arabian Sea (FAO Area 51) is a highly dynamic marine region governed by seasonal monsoon winds. This platform integrates physical variables (surface temperatures, salinity, currents, chlorophyll color) and biological indicators (fisheries catch index, larvae counts, cetacean sightings) to analyze cross-pillar coupling.

Key Finding: Strong coupling exists between primary productivity indicators (chlorophyll-a color) and subsequent fisheries landings, but seasonal upwellings introduce deep thermal buffering and migration lags.
